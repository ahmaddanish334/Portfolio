# Data Analytics & Data Science Portfolio

I am an MSc Econometrics student at TU Dortmund University with experience in data analysis, statistical modelling, machine learning, business intelligence, and applied econometrics.

This portfolio contains projects built with Python, R, SQL, Power BI, DAX, Power Query, MySQL, Scikit-learn, Statsmodels, BayesFlow, and spatial econometrics tools.

## Featured Projects

### 1. [Olist E-Commerce Analytics](./Power%20BI)

**Power BI · DAX · Power Query · SQL**

Built an end-to-end e-commerce business intelligence report covering sales performance, customer retention, delivery quality, product performance, seller performance, and freight costs.

Highlights:
- 99K+ orders and 112K+ order-item records
- 13.22M BRL delivered GMV
- four interactive Power BI report pages
- DAX measures for GMV, AOV, retention, reviews, delivery, freight, and time intelligence
- multi-table data model with Power Query transformations
- full `.pbix` file included

## Econometrics & Forecasting Projects

### [COVID-19 Forecasting Case Study](./COVID%20Forecasting%20Case%20Study)

**Python · Time Series · Machine Learning · Forecast Evaluation**

Three-part forecasting case study progressing from autoregressive models to machine learning and final forecast comparison.

Includes:
- AR, ARX, and seasonal models
- regression trees, MLP, and random forests
- rolling / expanding-window forecasting
- RMSFE and Diebold-Mariano tests
- comparison of 15 forecast series

---

### [The Fog of Crisis — Executive Communication](./Fog%20of%20Crisis%20-%20Executive%20Communication)

**Python · Panel Data · Fixed Effects · Difference-in-Differences**

Examined whether executive communication became more complex after COVID-19 using the Gunning Fog Index.

Includes:
- firm fixed-effects models
- time fixed effects
- clustered standard errors
- Difference-in-Differences analysis
- balanced-panel robustness checks
- sentiment, language, video, and firm-level controls

---

### [German Migration — Spatial Econometrics](./German%20Migration%20-%20Spatial%20Econometrics)

**R · Spatial Econometrics · GIS**

Analyzed net migration across 400 German districts and tested whether median income remains associated with migration after accounting for spatial dependence.

Includes:
- Moran's I
- OLS, SAR, SEM, and SDM models
- queen-contiguity spatial weights
- direct, indirect, and total impacts
- residual spatial-autocorrelation checks
- INKAR/BBSR and Eurostat/GISCO data

---

### [Simulation-Based Inference — Decision Modeling](./Simulation-Based%20Inference%20-%20Decision%20Modeling)

**Python · BayesFlow · Neural Networks · Bayesian Inference**

Extended the Racing Diffusion Model to study double responses in speeded decision tasks.

Includes:
- two RDM variants
- simulator-based synthetic training data
- DeepSet summary network
- CouplingFlow normalizing flow
- simulation-based calibration
- posterior contraction and parameter recovery
- posterior-predictive checks
